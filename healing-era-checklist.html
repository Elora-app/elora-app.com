The Healing Era
Checklist.

Small habits. Gentle shifts. A quiet return to yourself.

"Your healing era doesn't start with a dramatic moment. It starts with one small choice — to show up for yourself today."

A healing era isn't an aesthetic. It's a decision — to stop running from yourself and start returning to yourself. It doesn't have to look perfect. It just has to be real.

Use this checklist however feels right. Some things will resonate immediately. Others will take time. All of it is valid.

Mind — How You Think

I've stopped apologizing for how I feel
I'm learning to separate what happened from the story I'm adding to it
I'm becoming aware of my negative self-talk and actively working to soften it
I'm giving myself permission to not have everything figured out
I've started journaling — even just a few sentences a day
I'm learning to sit with discomfort instead of running from it
I'm noticing my emotional patterns — what triggers me, what restores me

Body — How You Care for Yourself

I'm drinking enough water and actually eating enough
I'm protecting my sleep — not scrolling until 2am every night
I'm moving my body in ways that feel good, not punishing
I've created a morning or evening ritual that's just for me
I'm learning to rest without guilt
I'm being gentler with my body — not criticizing, not comparing

Boundaries — How You Protect Your Peace

I've identified at least one thing I'm no longer available for
I'm practicing saying no without over-explaining myself
I'm distancing myself from people who drain me
I've unfollowed or muted accounts that make me feel worse about myself
I'm protecting my energy like it's valuable — because it is
I'm learning the difference between being alone and being lonely

Letting Go — What You're Releasing

I'm working on releasing guilt for things that weren't my fault
I'm letting go of who I thought I had to be
I'm releasing the need to understand everything before I can move forward
I'm not chasing people who have shown me they don't want to stay
I'm becoming okay with not having closure from everything
I'm letting the past inform me without letting it define me

Growth — What You're Building

I know what I want — even if I can't have it yet
I'm doing at least one thing every day that's just for me
I'm surrounding myself with things that inspire me and make me feel good
I'm investing in myself — through learning, reading, creating
I'm building a relationship with myself I'm actually proud of
I believe — even on hard days — that things can and will get better
"You don't have to check everything off this list to be in your healing era. Picking up the list is already the beginning."
