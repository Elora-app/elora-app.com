✦ ELORA JOURNAL PROMPTS

100 Journal Prompts
for self discovery, healing & growth.

Deep, honest prompts to help you understand yourself better. No rules. No judgment. Just you and the page.

Self Discovery
WHO ARE YOU WHEN NO ONE IS WATCHING?
01Who am I when I'm completely alone? How does she differ from who I am around others?
02What do I believe about myself that might not actually be true?
03What makes me feel most like myself?
04What are three things I love about myself that I rarely say out loud?
05What version of myself am I becoming? What does she look like?
06What do I keep doing that doesn't align with who I want to be?
07What did I used to love as a child that I've forgotten about?
08What would I do if I knew no one would judge me?
09What are my values? Am I living by them?
10What is the story I keep telling myself about who I am? Is it still true?
Healing & Processing
FOR WHAT YOU'RE STILL CARRYING
11What is something I'm still healing from that I haven't fully admitted to myself?
12What would I say to the younger version of me who went through that?
13What does healing feel like to me? What does it look like in my daily life?
14What am I still holding onto that I know I need to let go of?
15What has this painful experience taught me that nothing else could?
16Write a letter to the person who hurt you — not to send, just to release.
17What does forgiveness mean to me? Am I ready to explore it?
18What do I need to hear right now that no one is saying?
19What parts of myself did I lose in that experience? Can I reclaim them?
20What would it feel like to be fully healed from this?
Anxiety & Overthinking
FOR THE MIND THAT WON'T QUIET DOWN
21What am I most afraid of right now? Is that fear based on facts or assumptions?
22What is the worst case scenario I keep imagining? How likely is it really?
23What would I tell my best friend if she was feeling this anxious?
24What in my life is actually in my control right now? What isn't?
25What is one thing I can do today that will make tomorrow feel easier?
26What has worried me in the past that turned out to be okay?
27What story am I adding to what actually happened?
28What does my anxiety need right now — rest, movement, connection, or stillness?
29What would peace feel like in my body right now?
30Write down everything in your head right now. All of it. Leave it here.
Emotions & Mood
CHECKING IN WITH HOW YOU REALLY FEEL
31How am I really feeling today, beneath the surface?
32What emotion have I been avoiding lately? Why?
33What has been draining my energy this week?
34What has brought me unexpected joy recently?
35When did I last feel truly at peace? What was happening?
36What emotion do I find hardest to sit with? What happens when I try?
37What does my body feel like today? Where am I holding tension?
38What am I grateful for today, even on a hard day?
39What mood pattern have I noticed in myself lately?
40If my feelings today had a color, a weather, and a texture — what would they be?
Relationships
HOW YOU LOVE AND HOW YOU WANT TO BE LOVED
41What do I truly need in a relationship that I've never asked for?
42What patterns do I keep repeating in my relationships?
43What red flags have I ignored in the past? Why did I ignore them?
44What does a healthy relationship feel like to me?
45Is there a relationship in my life that no longer serves me?
46What boundaries do I need to set that I've been afraid to?
47How do I show up for others? How do I want others to show up for me?
48What would I do differently if I truly believed I deserved good love?
49What is the relationship with myself like right now?
50What kind of people do I want to attract into my life?
Growth & Goals
BUILDING THE LIFE YOU WANT
51What does my dream life actually look like? Be specific.
52What is one habit that would change everything for me if I did it consistently?
53What am I most proud of achieving in the last year?
54What fear is stopping me from going after what I actually want?
55What would I do if I knew I couldn't fail?
56What does success mean to me — not to anyone else, to me?
57Where do I want to be in 1 year? In 5 years?
58What am I tolerating in my life that I need to change?
59What skill do I want to learn and why haven't I started yet?
60What would the most disciplined version of me do today?
Gratitude & Presence
FINDING BEAUTY IN THE ORDINARY
61What are three small things that made today worth it?
62What is something I take for granted that I should appreciate more?
63When did I last feel truly present? What was I doing?
64What beauty existed in today that I almost missed?
65What is going right in my life that I forget to acknowledge?
66Who in my life am I most grateful for and why?
67What does a perfect ordinary day look like to me?
68What simple pleasure do I always forget how much I love?
69What am I looking forward to, even something small?
70If today was my last ordinary day, what would I want to remember about it?
Burnout & Rest
WHEN YOU'VE GIVEN TOO MUCH
71What has been draining me most lately? Is any of it in my control?
72What would genuine rest look like for me right now?
73What have I been saying yes to that I should say no to?
74What does my body need today that I've been ignoring?
75What pressure am I putting on myself that no one asked me to carry?
76What would I do today if productivity wasn't the goal?
77What am I protecting? Is it worth the energy it's costing me?
78What does recovery look like for me — not productivity, just recovery?
79When did I last feel genuinely rested? What made it possible?
80Write a permission slip to yourself. What are you allowing yourself to feel, do, or release today?
Hard Days
FOR WHEN EVERYTHING FEELS LIKE TOO MUCH
81What is actually happening right now, without the story I'm adding to it?
82What do I need right now — rest, to be heard, to cry, to move?
83What has helped me through hard days before?
84What would I say to a friend who was feeling exactly what I'm feeling?
85What is one small thing I can do right now to take care of myself?
86What am I making this mean about me that it doesn't actually mean?
87Will this matter in one year? In five?
88What hard day have I survived before that felt impossible at the time?
89What do I want tomorrow to feel like? What's one thing I can do to make that possible?
90Write freely. No structure. Just what's in your heart right now.
Dreams & Future Self
LETTERS TO WHO YOU ARE BECOMING
91Write a letter to your future self one year from now.
92What does your future self want you to know right now?
93What are you building right now that your future self will thank you for?
94What does your healed, whole self look like? How does she move through the world?
95What is one dream you've been too afraid to write down? Write it now.
96What would your life look like if you truly believed you deserved everything you wanted?
97What chapter of your life are you currently in? What is it teaching you?
98What do you want people to say about you when you leave a room?
99What is the one thing you most want to accomplish in this lifetime?
100Write a letter from your future healed, whole, thriving self — back to you, right now.
